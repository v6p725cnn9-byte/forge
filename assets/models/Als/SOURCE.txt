Mannequin.glb
  Mixamo X Bot (Beta) mannequin from three.js r170 examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb
  https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb
  Clips: idle, walk, run (plus agree, headShake, sad_pose, sneak_pose).
  Mixamo characters are royalty-free for use in games (Adobe Mixamo terms).
  Stand-in for ALS-Refactored SKM_Als, which ships only as an Unreal .uasset.
